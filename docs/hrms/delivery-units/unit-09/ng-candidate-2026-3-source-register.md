## External compliance questions

1. Supply or identify the canonical final National Minimum Wage (Amendment) Act 2024 gazette bytes and confirm the legally operative effective date for private employment-law compliance. The candidate uses 29 July 2024 only as a review boundary and refuses earlier periods.
2. For each supported State/FCT RTA, confirm the minimum-wage PAYE comparison basis for joiners, leavers, partial-year workers and irregular/non-periodic pay: actual monthly gross, expected annual gross, or another prescribed method.
3. For each supported RTA, specify how the PAYE attributable to a bonus, commission or other non-periodic payment is allocated to the payment period. JRB ss.13.1-13.2 require deduction/remittance but do not fully specify the numeric allocation method.

Until answered, affected calculations fail closed. No generic national or employer-location fallback exists.
