## Regulatory boundary

`NG-CANDIDATE-2026.3` remains `NOT_CERTIFIED`. Lagos, Oyo, and FCT non-periodic allocation and minimum-wage edge-case methods remain blocked pending RTA authority. No reviewer may type a guessed tax amount to resolve a hold. A resolution requires authoritative evidence, an approved effective rule, and deterministic recalculation.

## Operating flow

Frozen inputs → deterministic calculation → reconciliation → structured compliance eligibility → risk review → independent approval → finalization. Held employees remain linked to the original period and return through a governed correction, supplemental, retro, or off-cycle path; a finalized run is never rewritten.
