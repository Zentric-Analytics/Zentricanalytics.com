# Phase 4 page-identity audit

This audit was completed before Phase 4 implementation. It compares the four in-scope pages by purpose, composition, interaction, conversion, overlap, and distinctive elements. Recommendations are deliberately limited to page-specific refinements; shared tokens, navigation, footer, containers, typography, buttons, routes, and major functionality remain out of scope.

## Content-overlap audit

| Repeated concept | Home role | Services role | Industries role | Phase 4 decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Software engineering | High-level promise to build reliable digital products. | Detailed application, web, API, integration, and product-engineering capability. | Modernization and service-delivery outcome shaped by existing operations. | **refine** Home label and replace Industries service language; keep Services detail. |
| Artificial intelligence | Responsible application and orientation. | Models, retrieval, automation, integration, and governance-aware implementation. | Better decisions or service delivery under sector-specific governance. | **refine** Home and Industries framing; keep Services detail. |
| Data and analytics | Turning information into decisions. | Modeling, quality, KPI, reporting, and dashboard implementation. | Operational visibility and decision quality in context. | **refine** Home and Industries framing; keep Services detail. |
| Cloud and infrastructure | Modernizing foundations. | Named technology stack and implementation capability. | Resilience, continuity, scalability, and constraints. | **refine** Home and Industries framing; keep Services detail. |
| Research and innovation | Long-term experimentation with discipline. | Feasibility studies, prototypes, and experimental system design. | Adaptation where a sector or organization has novel constraints. | **refine** Home preview only; keep Services detail; avoid a generic Industries category. |
| Emerging technology | Careful exploration rather than novelty. | Practical evaluation and implementation options. | Only mention when connected to a concrete contextual outcome. | **refine** Home preview; keep Services detail; remove generic Industries duplication. |

## Intended page identities after Phase 4

- **Home — strategic and calm:** broad orientation, editorial philosophy, concise outcome-led capability preview, and one process interaction.
- **Services — technical and dense:** coded workspace, detailed capability catalogue, technology ticker, and one tabbed examples interaction.
- **Industries — contextual and navigable:** photographic context, one sector selector, outcome-led organizational support, and partnership trust.
- **Careers — human and scannable:** people photography, directly actionable roles, editorial values, and candidate-only conversion actions.

## Explicit deferrals

- **Phase 5:** page rhythm, color distribution, dark-section frequency, spacing cadence, and photographic art direction.
- **Phase 7:** comprehensive copywriting, deeper sector narratives, service-card prose polish, recruitment voice, and long-form philosophy/process editing.
