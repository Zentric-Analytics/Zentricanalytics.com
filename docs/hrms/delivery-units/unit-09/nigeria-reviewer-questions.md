# Nigeria qualified-review questions

## NG-CERT-Q01 — Employment-income and taxable-base membership
- Evidence: NG-SRC-001/002.
- Candidate: only explicitly mapped earning versions enter PAYE base.
- Alternative: category-specific inclusion/exemption/partial/ceiling rules.
- Impact: salary, hourly pay, overtime, leave, allowances, bonus, retro.
- Decision: annotate exact provisions and approve each row in the taxable-base matrix.

## NG-CERT-Q02 — Annual, periodic, and YTD PAYE method
- Evidence: NG-SRC-001/002.
- Candidate: cumulative taxable amount, progressive bands, prior YTD PAYE subtraction, negative result floored at zero.
- Alternative: different annualization/proration/refund allocation.
- Impact: every employee PAYE and retro.
- Decision: specify formula, tax year, periods, prior-pay handling, refunds, and official worked examples.

## NG-CERT-Q03 — Reliefs and allowable deductions
- Evidence: NG-SRC-001/002.
- Candidate: none invented; typed approved inputs required.
- Impact: PAYE base/liability.
- Decision: list formulae, eligibility, ceilings, evidence, timing, and effective dates.

## NG-CERT-Q04 — Pension coverage and basis
- Evidence: NG-SRC-004.
- Candidate: no enabled rates; separate employee and employer lines.
- Impact: net, employer cost, PAYE interaction, liabilities.
- Decision: annotate current coverage/exemptions, covered emoluments, percentages, employer-paid alternative, effective dates, and PAYE treatment.

## NG-CERT-Q05 — Rounding
- Evidence: exact official reference not yet annotated.
- Candidate: Decimal half-up at versioned scale.
- Impact: every monetary result, YTD, retro, journal.
- Decision: approve or replace mode/scale/stages/residual treatment.

## NG-CERT-Q06 — Proration and overtime constraints
- Evidence: Nigerian legal constraints plus Zentric policy.
- Candidate: explicit quantities/rates only; no universal denominator or overtime multiplier.
- Impact: hires, separations, unpaid leave, changes, hourly/overtime.
- Decision: reviewer identifies legal constraints; payroll-policy owner separately selects permissible operational conventions.

## NG-CERT-Q07 — Payslip content and correction
- Evidence: authoritative obligation/reference missing.
- Candidate: finalized-result-only immutable version with linked correction.
- Impact: employee disclosure and retention.
- Decision: required fields, timing, electronic delivery, YTD/contribution disclosure, corrected document treatment.

## NG-CERT-Q08 — Liability, remittance, and retention
- Evidence: NG-SRC-001/002/004 plus missing retention authority.
- Candidate: separate append-only liabilities and simulated remittance only; no asserted duration.
- Impact: reporting, acknowledgement, audit/archive.
- Decision: periods, due dates, outputs, correction process, acknowledgement, and each retention duration.
