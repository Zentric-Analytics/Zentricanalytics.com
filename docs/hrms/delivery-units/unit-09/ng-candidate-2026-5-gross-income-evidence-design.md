# Gross-employment-income evidence design

The frozen contract identifies employee, work relationship, payroll period, RTA, Salary, current Bonus, three tri-state ambiguity facts, other-taxable-income state, evidence references and certified-input version. Missing evidence never means `NO`.

`VERIFIED_NONE` permits the supported Salary/Bonus decision. `PRESENT` produces `OTHER_TAXABLE_EMPLOYMENT_INCOME_UNSUPPORTED`. `UNKNOWN` or incomplete certification produces `EMPLOYMENT_GROSS_INCOME_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`. No unsupported component is calculated or silently discarded.
