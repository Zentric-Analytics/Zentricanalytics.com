# Phase 7 content audit

Completed before Phase 7 copy edits. This audit treats Home as the preview, Services as the capability detail, Industries as contextual application, and About as company philosophy.

## Page-level audit

| Page | Primary audience and intent | Primary / secondary conversion | Main and supporting message | Gaps found |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Technology and business decision-makers assessing fit | Start a Project / Explore Services | Zentric is an engineering consultancy that improves operations through dependable software, data, and AI systems; disciplined process and collaboration support delivery | Hero led with a capability rather than company identity; primary and secondary CTAs were reversed; capability previews were not individually linked; philosophy and Services copy overlapped; “future-ready” was unsupported |
| Services | Buyers defining or modernizing a technology initiative | Start Your Project / Explore Industries | Zentric designs, builds, integrates, and supports technology for specific users and outcomes | Cards named outputs but rarely identified audiences or outcomes; technology section listed tools without selection rationale; examples did not consistently distinguish challenge, approach, and outcome; consultation CTA duplicated the primary destination |
| Industries | Leaders evaluating sector and operating-context fit | Discuss Your Needs / Explore Services | Delivery adapts to users, regulation, governance, workflows, and measures of success | Hero claimed “every industry”; sector copy leaned on broad organization types; “How We Help” repeated service benefits; trust claims needed mechanisms; Services link was missing |
| Careers | Candidates assessing roles and culture | Apply Now / Track Application | Join a team that values ownership, learning, communication, quality, and respect | Hero was a long list; respect was implicit rather than explicit; no application-process explanation; repeated Apply CTAs competed without adding information |
| About | Prospective clients and partners validating company fit | Talk to Our Team / Explore Services | Zentric exists to make consequential technology easier to operate and improve through careful engineering and direct collaboration | Page was four brief marketing cards; story, leadership model, and working approach were missing; no CTA or internal link; service catalogue was repeated |

## Duplication and trust findings

- **Software, AI, data, cloud, research:** Home should preview outcomes; Services owns build detail and technologies; Industries explains contextual constraints; About explains why and how Zentric makes decisions.
- **Repeated benefits:** “secure, scalable, reliable” appeared without nearby mechanisms. Revised copy should name practices such as access boundaries, testing, review points, maintainable architecture, staged delivery, and operational handover—or use measured language.
- **Generic language:** “every industry,” “building the future,” “lasting impact,” “right solution,” “innovation,” and “future-ready” should be removed or grounded in a specific outcome.
- **Proof:** No certifications, client statistics, named case studies, or leadership biographies are available. Do not invent them. Detailed case studies, verified credentials, and named leadership profiles are **DEFERRED** pending approved source material.

## SEO and accessibility findings

- Root metadata was generic and shared by every route; canonical and Open Graph metadata were absent.
- Existing scoped pages generally had one H1 and logical H2/H3 structure. Service cards incorrectly repeated H2 beneath the section H2 and should become H3.
- Privacy and Terms routes referenced by the footer were absent.
- Existing CTA labels were mostly meaningful, but “Let’s Talk,” repeated anchor prompts, and unlinked Home capability rows weakened journey clarity.
- Preserve semantic sections, descriptive controls, focus behavior, reduced-motion behavior, form labels, validation messaging, and image alternatives.

## Deferred review

- Legal counsel should review Privacy and Terms copy, establish the controlling legal entity/jurisdiction, and approve retention periods.
- Approved client evidence, case studies, certifications, and leadership biographies would strengthen proof but are unavailable in the repository.
- The footer placeholder email and broad footer tagline need a future owner-approved update; Phase 7 explicitly excludes footer changes.
